An indigenous nursery in the middle of town

We grow plants that love the Garden Route coast: salt wind, summer sun and all. Ask us what will thrive in your garden and we'll walk you to it.

    The Farm Shop pantry

    Grains, pulses, seeds and spices sold by weight, beside shelves of homemade preserves, rusks and biscuits. Less packaging, more flavour.

    1. Bring a clean jar or bagOr buy one of ours at the counter.
    2. We weigh it emptySo you only pay for what goes inside.
    3. Fill upRice, couscous, oats, lentils, nuts and more.
    4. Pay by weightCard or cash, then pick a cookie for the road.
